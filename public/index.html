Commercial API Endpoints

Ultra-fast sub-50ms FMCSA intelligence running on Vercel Serverless Edge.

GET /v1/carrier/lookup

Complete carrier record: operating authority, fleet size, drivers, safety rating, insurance audit, fraud risk score, and federal docket links.

Params: ?dot=54283 or ?mc=135684
GET /v1/carrier/risk-score

Automated fraud prevention engine: 0-100 risk score, chameleon carrier detection (<90 days), virtual address flags, and load tendering recommendation.

Params: ?dot=54283
GET /v1/carrier/insurance

Deep insurance compliance audit: verifies $750k/$1M BIPD active status, cargo threshold, $75k broker bond, and pending cancellation notices.

Params: ?dot=54283
GET /v1/carrier/safety

24-month inspection records, vehicle and driver Out-of-Service (OOS) percentages benchmarked directly against US national averages, and crash counts.

Params: ?dot=54283
GET /v1/carrier/docket

Direct 1-click links to official government verification portals: FMCSA SAFER Snapshot, SMS Safety Profile, and Licensing & Insurance (L&I).

Params: ?dot=54283
GET /v1/carrier/stats

Current US national safety averages (vehicle 22.26%, driver 6.67%), statutory insurance thresholds, and complete fraud rule catalogue.

Params: None (Public metadata)
